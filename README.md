Adam Bettayeb, electrical engineering student at Texas A&M, graduating May 2028.

I'm interested in power and energy systems. This semester I'm doing undergraduate research with the UrbanResilience.AI Lab mapping U.S. data centers, which are becoming one of the largest new loads on the grid.

### Projects

[Op-amp active filters](https://github.com/AdamBettayeb06/analog-active-filters). A Sallen-Key and a first-order low-pass filter designed to 1 kHz specs, simulated in LTspice, then built and measured with my lab partner. The writeup covers where the hardware didn't match the model and why.

[Single-cycle LEGv8 processor](https://github.com/AdamBettayeb06/legv8-single-cycle-cpu). A 64-bit ARMv8-subset CPU in Verilog: register file, ALU, sign extender, branch logic, and control unit, with a test program that runs a loop and memory operations to completion.

[Verilog state machines on an FPGA](https://github.com/AdamBettayeb06/verilog-fsm-fpga). A traffic light controller and a combination lock, run on a Zybo board and checked on an oscilloscope.

[TrojanShield](https://github.com/AdamBettayeb06/trojanshield). Team project from the Aggie Invent 2026 competition, a tool for catching dormant firmware trojans. I worked on the initial concept, including the idea of checking chips' power and timing traces for signs of tampering.

Tools I've used: Verilog, Vivado, ARM assembly, LTspice, Python, oscilloscopes, and Autodesk Inventor.
